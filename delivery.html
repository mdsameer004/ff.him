Delivery Information

At Friends Florist, we ensure every floral arrangement reaches you fresh, beautiful, and on time—because every moment deserves perfection.

We provide quick and dependable delivery for all floral arrangements, ensuring your flowers arrive in perfect condition and full bloom.

Delivery Time:

Orders are usually delivered within hours after confirmation. We prioritize freshness and timely service for every order.

Delivery Areas:

We currently deliver within the city and nearby areas. Our service is expanding to bring flowers closer to more customers every day.

Delivery Charges:

Delivery charges may vary depending on the distance. Exact charges will be shared during order confirmation.

Same Day Delivery:

Same day delivery is available for orders placed before 11 PM. Perfect for last-minute surprises and special occasions.

For urgent orders please contact us directly.
