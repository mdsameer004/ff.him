Returns & Refunds Policy

Our commitment to freshness and quality service.

Due to the perishable nature of flowers, we generally do not accept returns.

However, if you receive damaged or incorrect products, please contact us within 24 hours of delivery.

Refund Eligibility:

  • Wrong product delivered
  • Damaged flowers
  • Order not delivered

After verification, we will either replace the product or issue a refund.

For refund requests contact our support team.
