Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Friends Florist.

1. How can I place an order?

You can order directly from our website by selecting a product and completing checkout.

2. Do you provide same day delivery?

Yes, orders placed before 2 PM are eligible for same day delivery.

3. What payment methods are accepted?

We accept UPI, credit/debit cards and online payments.

4. Can I customize flower arrangements?

Yes, custom orders are available on request.

5. How can I contact customer support?

You can contact us through the contact page or phone number provided.
