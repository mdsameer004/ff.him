Our Story

Discover the passion and history behind Friends Florist.

A Legacy of Blooms Since 1999

Friends Florist began with a simple belief: flowers have a unique language of their own, capable of expressing emotions when words fall short. Established in 1999 in the heart of Hyderabad, what started as a small neighborhood flower shop has gracefully bloomed into a premier destination for luxury botanical arrangements.

Our journey has been guided by an unwavering commitment to quality, creativity, and the joy of our customers. Over the decades, we have partnered with local growers and international farms to source only the freshest, most vibrant blooms. Whether it's a grand wedding, a tender anniversary, or a simple gesture of love, our expert florists pour their hearts into crafting arrangements that tell a beautiful story.

Today, as we continue to grow, our core values remain rooted in the community. We are not just selling flowers; we are helping you celebrate life's most precious moments. Step into our world of elegance and let nature's finest creations bring a touch of magic to your doorstep.
